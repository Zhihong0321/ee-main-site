    12Quick answers

    Before you sign

    Could solar suit my home?

    There is no single bill threshold. Daytime electricity use, roof space, shading, system design and the installed price all affect value. A site-specific estimate should show its assumptions.

    What should I compare in two quotes?

    Compare system size, panel and inverter models, mounting, protection, installation scope, warranty terms, estimated generation, total price and exclusions.

    Who supports a warranty claim?

    Check the warranty provider, claim contact and local service responsibility for each item. Eternalgy lists its registration details and links to official directories so you can verify them.

    Do I need a battery?

    A battery is optional. Compare its added cost with your evening electricity use and whether backup power matters to you.

    Can I install solar on a condo?

    Rooftop access and approval depend on strata ownership, building rules and current programme requirements. Check with your management and a registered provider before assuming eligibility.

    Already have a quote?

    Send it on WhatsApp. We can help review the equipment, installation scope, coverage and assumptions side by side.
