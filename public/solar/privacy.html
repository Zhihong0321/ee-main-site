Privacy Notice

This notice explains how Eternalgy Sdn Bhd handles the personal data you give us through this website, as required by the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA). A Bahasa Malaysia version follows below.

1. Who we are

Eternalgy Sdn Bhd (Company No. 202301029164 / 1523087-A), 21-01, Jalan Mutiara Emas 10/19, Taman Mount Austin, 81100 Johor Bahru, Johor, Malaysia (“Eternalgy”, “we”). We are the data user for the information collected on this page.

2. Personal data we collect

  • What you submit: your name, mobile/WhatsApp number, monthly electricity bill range, property type, state, and whether you already have a solar quote.
  • Technical and campaign data: IP address, device and browser type, pages visited, and advertising identifiers such as the Google click ID (GCLID) and campaign parameters (for example, which advert brought you here).

3. Why we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry, arrange a site survey and prepare a solar proposal.
  • To contact you by WhatsApp, phone call or SMS about that enquiry.
  • To measure and improve the performance of our website and advertising.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

If you decide to proceed, a Solar ATAP application to SEDA Malaysia and TNB requires further documents (for example identity card and TNB account details). We collect those separately, with a separate notice at that stage.

4. Who we share it with

Our staff and area representatives; service providers that help us run this website and our messaging and advertising measurement (including Google); SEDA Malaysia, TNB and other authorities when you instruct us to apply on your behalf; and anyone we are required to disclose to by law. We do not sell your personal data.

5. How long we keep it

We keep personal data only for as long as necessary for the purposes above, or as the law requires.

6. Your rights

You may ask to access or correct your personal data, withdraw your consent to be contacted, or limit how we process it. Contact us using the details in section 8. If you withdraw consent we will stop contacting you, although we may need to keep a record that you did.

7. Cookies and tracking

We use the Google tag to measure how our advertising performs. You can control or delete cookies in your browser settings; the page will still work.

8. Contact us

WhatsApp or call +60 11-2100 0099, or write to our Johor Bahru office at the address above.
