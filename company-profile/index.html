Eternalgy Corporate Profile 20264

Your bill after solar,
simulated from the tariff.

Most solar quotes multiply your bill by a saving percentage. The Eternalgy Solar Simulator, built in-house by our software team, does the arithmetic instead: it rebuilds your TNB bill from the tariff, follows every kilowatt-hour your system generates, and calculates the bill you will pay after solar, item by item. For homes and for businesses.

Your bill

The ringgit amount and the month on your TNB bill.

Tariff match

The usage that produces that bill under TNB's tariff, with that month's surcharge.

System size

Panels sized to your usage at Malaysian sun-peak hours.

Energy flow

Used on site, exported, or beyond the credit cap.

New bill

Every component recalculated at your new usage.

Your saving

Old bill minus new bill, plus the export credit.

What the engine models

  • TNB's residential tariff in force since 1 July 2025, line by line: energy, capacity, network and retail charges
  • TNB's low-voltage commercial tariff
  • Sixteen months of fuel surcharge (AFA), July 2025 to October 2026
  • SST and retail-charge thresholds, and the energy efficiency incentive (EEI) bands
  • ATAP export rules: credit capped at what is imported, no roll-over
  • Battery capacity, depth of discharge and charging losses
  • Commercial load, hour by hour, against opening hours

Why the method matters

Solar is a 25-year asset. A saving overstated by RM150 a month is not a small error: it repeats every month the system runs.

Eternalgy Corporate Profile 20267

Why the numbers
hold up.

It starts from the real bill

The bill is matched to the usage that produces it under TNB's current tariff, so the simulation knows the usage instead of guessing it from a percentage.

It applies the bill month's surcharge

The fuel surcharge (AFA) changes every month. Using the one on the bill stops the same ringgit being read as the wrong usage.

It rebuilds the bill after solar

Energy, network, capacity, retail, SST, KWTBB and the efficiency rebate are recalculated at the new usage. Tariff bands and tax thresholds fall away as they would on a real bill.

It follows every kilowatt-hour

Solar used on site saves the full tariff. Exported solar earns only the export rate, only up to what is imported that month; the rest expires, as under ATAP.

It models business hours

Commercial premises are simulated hour by hour against opening hours and round-the-clock load, so a shop closed on Sunday shows a smaller saving.

It shows its working

Every component is on screen, before and after. The customer can check the arithmetic, and so can their bank.

What a simulation cannot know

Weather and season, the roof's direction and shading, changes in how much power is used, and future tariffs and rules. The simulator gives a detailed forecast, not a guarantee. We confirm it with a site survey and, ideally, up to twelve months of bills before you commit.
