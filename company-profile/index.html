Duo Mix

String inverters on large, uniform roof areas and micro inverters on shaded or complex sections, in a single system. Each roof section gets the right conversion technology: higher yield, less single-point dependence and panel-level data.

Sole distributor for SAJ in Malaysia, with warehouses in Johor and Selangor for fast replacement. Our in-house software engineering runs cloud invoicing, cloud inventory, automated stock-keeping, scheduling and fully digital reporting.
